Drag the bridge along the string, or click where you want it. The two lengths, and their ratio in lowest terms, update as you move — and each length can be plucked so you can *hear* the ratio. Try the halfway point (1:1, the same note twice), then two-fifths of the way (3:2, a fifth), and listen to "Pluck both". This is the arithmetic behind the [[monochord]].
