The instruments behind the notes: how real strings and keys turn pitch into something you can touch. Each note ends with a short history of the instrument.

- [[monochord]] — one string and a movable bridge: the ruler for ratios.
- [[lute]] — a plucked, fretted instrument whose movable gut frets make tuning a choice.
- [[guitar]] — where every pitch class sits on a fretted fingerboard.
- [[piano]] — where every pitch class sits on the keys.
- [[viola]] — a fretless string instrument tuned in fifths.
