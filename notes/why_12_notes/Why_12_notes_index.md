Why does an octave hold twelve notes, and not ten or fifteen? This section collects the pieces of that answer. Start with [[why_12_notes]] — stack twelve pure fifths on a string and watch the chromatic scale fall out, along with the small leftover gap (the Pythagorean comma) that no arithmetic can close. Then try [[monochord]] to hear the ratios behind it, and follow the gap forward into [[Tuning_systems]] to see how each era chose to hide it.

Notes on the twelve-note question will be added to this directory as they are written.
